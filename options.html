Social Media Downloader - Options

When are cookies needed?

Cookies are required for login-gated platforms like Threads, Instagram, and Facebook to bypass video availability restrictions.

How to export cookies:

  • Chrome: Install the "EditThisCookie" extension, visit the platform site, export cookies in Netscape format
  • Edge: Install the "EditThisCookie" extension, visit the platform site, export cookies in Netscape format
  • Firefox: Install the "Cookie Editor" add-on, visit the platform site, export cookies in Netscape format
